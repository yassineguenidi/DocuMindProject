# DocuMind AI

**DocuMind AI is an intelligent document automation platform designed to help businesses extract, structure, validate, and manage information from documents automatically.**

The platform combines **OCR, document classification, information extraction, and AI-powered processing** to transform unstructured documents such as invoices, contracts, forms, and business documents into structured and actionable data.

DocuMind AI provides a modern SaaS environment where users can upload documents, monitor processing activity, track usage and quotas, review extracted information, and manage their documents through a centralized dashboard.

### Key Features

* **Secure user authentication** with sign-up and sign-in
* **Document upload and management**
* **Automated document classification**
* **OCR-based text extraction**
* **AI-powered information extraction**
* **Structured data generation** in formats such as JSON and Excel
* **Document processing and validation workflows**
* **Usage tracking and quota management**
* **Dashboard with document and processing statistics**
* **User and company management**
* **API-ready architecture for integration with external applications**
* Future integrations with **ERP, CRM, and business information systems**

### Technology Stack

The project is built around a modern architecture using technologies such as **React, TypeScript, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Python, OCR technologies, machine learning, and AI/LLM-based document processing**.

The architecture is designed to support both a **SaaS deployment model** and, in the future, **on-premise deployments** for organizations with specific security or data-management requirements.

### Target Market

DocuMind AI is primarily designed for **French SMEs and business professionals** who handle large volumes of documents and want to reduce manual data-entry and processing tasks.

The initial target market focuses on **real estate**, with potential extensions to **recruitment and HR, accounting, insurance, healthcare, and other document-intensive industries**.

### Vision

The goal of DocuMind AI is to turn documents from static files into **structured business data**, reducing repetitive manual work, improving data quality, accelerating document processing, and helping companies make better use of their information.

Ultimately, DocuMind AI aims to become a flexible **AI-powered document automation platform** capable of integrating seamlessly into existing business workflows.
